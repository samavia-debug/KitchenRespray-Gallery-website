# Respray Photos: staff guide

For Joanna and Sajjad. This covers everything you need for day-to-day work on the portfolio website: adding projects, photos, videos, reviews and brochures, and sending customers a link to the right gallery.

You cannot break the website from your login. You only see the parts you need, and nothing appears on the public site until you press **Publish**.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to the website address followed by `/wp-admin` (for example `resprayphotos.com/wp-admin`).
2. Enter your own username and password.
3. You land on the **Projects** list.

The menu on the left has: **Projects**, **Videos**, **Reviews**, **Brochures**, **Media** and **Profile**.

---

## 2. Adding a new project

A project is one finished job. It gets its own page on the website and appears automatically in every gallery it belongs to.

1. Click **Projects**, then **Add New Project**.
2. Type a **title** that describes the job, e.g. *Oak to Matt Black Shaker Kitchen*.
3. In the large box you can write a short **description** (two or three sentences). This is optional.
4. Add the **photos** (see section 3).
5. On the right, choose the **Colour** (see section 4).
6. On the right, tick the **Service**, and the **Style** and **Finish** if you know them. Material and Paint brand are optional.
7. Fill in **Project details** if you have them (see section 5).
8. Press **Publish**.

If something required is missing, the project is saved as a draft and a red message at the top tells you what to add. Add it and press **Publish** again.

**Required before publishing:** a title, a main colour, a service, and at least one photo.

**Not ready yet?** Press **Save Draft**. Press **Preview** to see how the page will look before it goes live.

### What the website does for you

When you publish, the website automatically:

- creates the project's own page,
- adds it to the main gallery and to every matching collection (for example a black kitchen appears in *Black Kitchens*),
- makes it findable in the search,
- makes small, fast-loading versions of each photo,
- adds a description to each photo for Google,
- shows it under "Related projects" on similar jobs.

You never need to upload the same project twice.

## 3. Photos

The **Project photos** box has three sections:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Before photos** | How it looked before the work |
| **After photos** | The finished result |
| **More photos** | Close-ups, details, anything else |

To add photos:

1. Click the button under the section, e.g. **Add after photos**.
2. Drag photos from your computer into the window, or click **Select Files**. You can choose many at once.
3. Click **Add to project**.

Then:

- **Reorder** by dragging a photo left or right.
- **Move** a photo to a different section by dragging it there.
- **Remove** a photo from the project with the red **×** (the photo stays in the Media library).

**Main photo.** This is the picture shown on the gallery card. If you do not choose one (box on the right, *Main photo*), the first After photo is used.

**Before and after slider.** If a project has at least one Before photo and one After photo, the website shows a drag-to-compare slider using the *first* photo of each. For the best effect, make those two photos the same view of the same room. Put the pair you want compared first in each section.

### Photo tips

- Use the original photo from the phone or camera, not a screenshot or a picture forwarded several times on WhatsApp (those lose quality).
- Use plain photos. Do not add text, logos, phone numbers or "Before/After" labels onto the picture; the website adds labels itself.
- Landscape (wide) photos look best in the gallery.
- Do not include people, house numbers, car registrations or anything that identifies the customer's home.
- If a yellow message says photos are identical to ones already in the library, check you have not uploaded the same picture twice.

## 4. Colour

In the **Colour** box on the right:

1. Choose the **Main colour** (required). This decides which colour gallery the project appears in.
2. If the job is two-tone, tick the second colour under **Other colours**.

Pick the closest colour group. For example, a dark charcoal kitchen is **Anthracite** or **Grey**; an off-white is **White** or **Cream**. The exact paint name goes in *New colour name* under Project details.

You choose from fixed lists so that everyone uses the same names. If a colour, style or service you need is missing, ask the website administrator to add it.

---

## 5. Project details

All optional, but each one makes the project page more useful.

| Field | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Original colour | Oak | What it was before |
| New colour name | Farrow & Ball Railings | The exact paint colour |
| Location | Bray | Town or area only. Never a street address |
| Completion date | 14 September 2026 | Lets customers filter by year |
| Video link | A YouTube or Vimeo link | Shows the video on the project page |
| Featured project | Tick the box | Puts the project on the homepage |
| Staff notes | Anything useful | Private. Never shown on the website |

### Writing the description with AI (if switched on)

If the administrator has switched it on, a **Generate Project Description with AI** button appears under the description box.

1. Fill in the title, colour, service and any project details first. Put anything worth mentioning in **Staff notes** (e.g. "island resprayed a darker shade, new handles fitted by customer").
2. Click the button and wait up to half a minute.
3. **Read the text.** It only knows what you typed. Correct anything that is wrong and delete anything you are not sure is true.
4. Press **Publish** or **Update** as usual.

The button never publishes anything by itself. If it shows an error, just write the description by hand.

## 6. Editing or removing a project

1. Click **Projects**. Use the search box, or the **All colours / All services / All finishes** filters at the top, to find it.
2. Click the title to open it.
3. Make your changes and press **Update**.

To take a project off the website without deleting it, open it, change **Status** to *Draft* and press **Update**. To delete it, use **Move to Trash** (it can be restored from Trash for 30 days).

The list shows a red **Missing** where a project has no colour or service, so gaps are easy to spot.

---

## 7. Adding many projects at once (Bulk add)

Use this when you have lots of finished jobs with one photo each.

1. Click **Projects**, then **Bulk add**.
2. Click **Upload or choose photos** and select all the photos.
3. Type a title, and choose the colour, service, style and finish they share.
4. Click **Create draft projects**.

Each photo becomes its own **draft** project, numbered 1, 2, 3. Nothing is public yet. Open each draft, give it a proper title, check the details, and press **Publish**.

---

## 8. Videos

Videos are stored on YouTube or Vimeo, not on the website, so the site stays fast.

1. Upload the video to the company YouTube or Vimeo account and copy its link.
2. Click **Videos**, then **Add video**.
3. Type a title and, if you like, a short description.
4. Paste the link into **Video link**.
5. Tick a **Category** on the right.
6. If the video shows a project that is on the website, paste that project's web address into **Related project**.
7. Press **Publish**.

The thumbnail comes from YouTube automatically. To use your own, set a featured image.

---

## 9. Reviews

Only add genuine reviews, word for word as the customer wrote them.

1. Click **Reviews**, then **Add review**.
2. **Title:** the customer's name as it should appear (e.g. *Mary K.*).
3. **Main box:** the customer's words.
4. Choose the **Star rating** and where the review was left (Google, Facebook, email).
5. **Related project:** only paste a project address if the customer agreed to their photos being shown with their review.
6. Press **Publish**.

---

## 10. Brochures

1. Click **Brochures**, then **Add brochure or resource**.
2. Type a title and a short description.
3. Click **Choose or upload file** and pick the PDF.
4. Tick a **Category**.
5. For a cover picture, set the featured image on the right (a picture of the front page works well).
6. Press **Publish**.

Customers can download brochures without signing up.

---

## 11. Sending a customer the right gallery

This is the quickest way to answer "Have you done any black kitchens?"

**Ready-made collections.** These addresses always work and are easy to remember:

- `/gallery/black-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/white-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/grey-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/cream-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/green-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/blue-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/shaker-kitchens/`
- `/gallery/kitchens/`, `/gallery/wardrobes/`, `/gallery/furniture/`, `/gallery/commercial/`, `/gallery/doors/`, `/gallery/bathrooms/`
- `/gallery/matt-finishes/`, `/gallery/gloss-finishes/`

**Any other combination.**

1. Open the website and click **Gallery**.
2. Type what the customer asked for in the search box (e.g. *grey shaker kitchen matt*), or tick the filters.
3. Check the results look right.
4. Click **Copy Gallery Link**.
5. Paste the link into WhatsApp, text or email.

The customer sees exactly the same projects you saw, on any phone or computer, with no login.

To share a single project, open it and use **Copy link** or the WhatsApp button under *Share this project*.

---

## 12. Gallery pages (collections)

Under **Projects → Gallery pages** you will find the ready-made collections from section 11. You can add a new one when customers keep asking for the same thing:

1. Click **Add gallery page**.
2. Title it, e.g. *Navy Kitchens*. The address is made from the title (`/gallery/navy-kitchens/`).
3. Write one or two sentences of introduction in the main box.
4. Tick what the projects must match (e.g. Colour: Navy Blue, Service: Kitchen Respray).
5. Press **Publish**.

The page fills itself with every matching project, now and in future.

---

## 13. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Saved as a draft, not published" | Read the red message; it lists what is missing. Add it and press Publish again |
| A project is not in the gallery you expected | Open it and check the Main colour and Service |
| No before/after slider on a project | It needs at least one Before photo and one After photo |
| A photo is sideways | Rotate it on your phone or computer first, save, and upload it again |
| Upload fails | The file may be too large or not a photo. Try a smaller JPG |
| You cannot find a menu or setting | It is probably administrator-only. Ask the website administrator |
| Forgot your password | Click "Lost your password?" on the login page |
